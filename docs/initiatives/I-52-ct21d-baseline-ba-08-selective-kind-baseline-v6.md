## 1. Scope linkage

The kind covers exactly the scope of BA-03 V5: `SEL-FRONTAL` (one view per fondo `k`, `0 <= k < SelectiveDepthLayout.Count(system)`) and `SEL-PLANTA` (one whole-rack view). **The lateral and the legacy frontal `Section = -1` (`SEL-LEGACY-M1`) are out; the planta, whose canonical section is `-1`, is in** (BA08-10). `Count(system) = Math.Max(1, system?.DepthCount ?? 1)` (`Application/Systems/Selective/SelectiveDepthLayout.cs:18`); `MaxDepthCount = 4` (`Domain/Systems/Selective/SelectiveRackDefaults.cs:40`). (Both blobs are byte-identical at the bound SHA: `UNCHANGED`.)

**Legacy blank logical `Name` (FXB-06).** A source whose logical `Name` is blank is **in scope as a legacy product state** (AR5-11; the scope line is BA-03 V5's): at the bound SHA no product route creates a new Selective rack with a blank name, but the exact build preserves one (section 4.1). This is distinct from the V2.1 25.4 exclusion, which is literal to `Section = -1` (canonicalized before planning); a blank `Name` reaches planning verbatim and drives the mirror's blank-base name path (`Rack - espejo`, section 15).

### 12.1 Completeness by authored output (D-03), bound SHA and exact build (AR5-14; IA-G-02)

**Bound SHA and exact build are separate (AR6-01, AR5-12; IA-G-02).** The editor inputs, their initial values and the authored-field rules of sections 12.1 to 12.3 are **facts at the bound SHA** (`RSW` blobs `199de141` and `0724d32c`, `SES` `7096c310`; section 3.2). A fixture instance is **drawn by the exact build** (the `PRODUCT_BUILD`), with one exception: step 1 of `FX-ANN-BLANK` (below). Bound SHA and exact build agree through the AR5-12 identity precondition only: the (path, blob) pairs of section 3.2 at the source SHA of the build that draws the instance equal those at the bound SHA, or the delta is recorded and processed (section 16.3 K-11). The `FIXTURE_CONSTRUCTION_BUILD` is outside that precondition (FXB-03); its relation to the bound SHA is recorded instead (below and section 12.6).

A fixture is specified by its **editor inputs** in the Selective editor for a **new rack**: the inputs of sections 12.2 and 12.3 are set, and every other input keeps its initial value at the bound SHA. The fixture's design is the **authored output** of those inputs, the `SelectivePalletDesign` that `SelectiveEditorState.BuildDesign` writes (`SES:1327-1400`; `SES` byte-identical at the bound SHA), **not** the defaults of the domain type. ## 15. Naming and identity

`NewRackId` is minted once per logical mirrored rack by the orchestrator and is identical in every sibling envelope. Effective view names are computed in PREPARE-R under the lock and returned by the seams. **Nested names (BA06-F11):** the requested names restart per plan and the effective names depend on the block table at the call; the orchestrator therefore calls SL-1 in **ascending `k`**, then SL-2, and every seam returns the handles of **all** objects it created (nested definitions and SL-Y layers included), from which the sealed list takes the effective names and phase A its domain (section 5). The final mirror name is an I-52 authority (V17: `<base> - espejo`, never blank; the reused I-51 allocator falls back to `Rack` for a blank base, `Application/Persistence/RackDuplicationPlan.cs:127,421`, blob `e939f4d0` byte-identical at the bound SHA), not fixed here.

**Naming ruling (decisions section 223.1: `NAMING_RULING = NAMING_COMPATIBLE_NO_CHANGE`).** Proposal V17 PDC-2 (`<base> - espejo`, no ordinal, never blank; `Rack - espejo` for a blank base), ID-4, section 9 and section 10.1 step 5, inherited by the Freeze V18, **govern** the mirror's logical name; it is I-52's own authority, and this artifact records it without fixing it. I-60 (the automatic `Selectivo N`) is **not** a system-wide authority: its Freeze sections 4 and 5 and its decision apply it only to the new-rack routes they list and exclude copy-provenance operations (duplication, layout, projection). RACKMIRROR is a copy-provenance creation (V17 section 9 reuses the I-51 assigner with an injected name policy), so it is **outside I-60's scope** with no exception needed.
