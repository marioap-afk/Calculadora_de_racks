### 4.3 Recorded consequence: nested effective names (BA06-F11)

The requested nested names restart per plan (`SEL_FRONTAL_<Role>_<n>`, `PLANTA_CAB_<n>`) and the effective names come from `UniqueBlockName` against the block table **at the moment of the call** (evidence `sa3b` F5.1, F5.2). When several frontals are created in one `T_M`, the suffixes therefore depend on the creation order (F5.4, a host fact to be confirmed by the trace). **Rule (`Ops(plan)`, section 11):** the orchestrator calls SL-1 in **ascending `k`**, then SL-2, then SL-4 per view; every seam returns the handles of all objects it created, nested definitions included (C-rows of 15.4), and the EVM selector reads the effective nested names from those handles. Nested names are not a separate contract output (DC log, resolution of R-06).

## 7. Dynamic-trace plan (conditions 3 and 4; submitted for approval together with `STATIC_REVIEWED`, AR3-22)

**Method (chosen).** Each trace is a **non-governing dry run** on the implemented seams, in a scratch copy of the fixture of its plan-shape class (section 10.2), on the **characterization build** of the exact build (AR3-01: a build of the product with harness-only switches; its identity is the run tuple field `CHARACTERIZATION_BUILD`).
